# Ocean Voyage

A kid-friendly browser game inspired by Polynesian seafaring (and a certain ocean-loving Disney movie). Sail your outrigger canoe across a bright blue ocean, collect starfish, shells, pearls, and coral, then bring them home to your island to sell and upgrade your boat.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no install.

```
open index.html
```

## Controls

- **Arrow keys / WASD** — sail your boat
- **Click / tap the water** — sail to that spot
- Glide over an item to scoop it into your cargo hold
- Sail back to your **Home Island** to sell your haul and buy upgrades
- A compass arrow appears when you wander far from home

## Items

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Shell | 3 gold |
| Starfish | 6 gold |
| Coral | 8 gold |
| Pearl | 12 gold |

## Upgrades

- **Cargo Hold** — carry more items per trip
- **Faster Sail** — zip across the ocean
- **Stronger Hull** — bigger, sturdier boat
- **Lucky Net** — wider collection range

Upgrade prices scale with each level — save up for the rarer pearls!
