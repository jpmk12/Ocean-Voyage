# Ocean Voyage

A kid-friendly browser game inspired by Polynesian seafaring. Sail your outrigger canoe across a bright ocean, collect treasures, befriend sea creatures, discover hidden islands, and play mini-games to earn gold.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no install.

```
open index.html
```

## Controls

- **Arrow keys / WASD** — sail your boat
- **Click / tap the water** — sail to that spot
- Glide over items to scoop them up
- Sail near a wild dolphin or sea turtle and **press F (or tap them)** to befriend
- Return to any island to sell cargo, upgrade, or play a mini-game

## Items

| Item | Value | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Shell | 3 gold | Common |
| Starfish | 6 gold | Common |
| Coral | 8 gold | Uncommon |
| Pearl | 12 gold | Uncommon |
| **Golden Conch** | **50 gold** | Rare — glows in the water |
| **Treasure Chest** | **200 gold** | Legendary — full of pirate gold! |

Rare items appear every 10-20 seconds near a random island. Volcano Island favors Golden Conches; Pearl Atoll favors Treasure Chests.

## Islands

- **Home Island** — sell, upgrade, and play **Coconut Catch**
- **Reef Atoll** — coral-rich waters, play **Fishing**
- **Volcano Island** *(unlocks at Faster Sail Lv 2)* — Golden Conches wash up here
- **Pearl Atoll** *(unlocks at Lucky Net Lv 2)* — pearls and treasure chests galore

Locked islands appear as misty silhouettes on the map until you've upgraded enough to reach them.

## Sea Creatures

Wild dolphins and turtles swim around the ocean. Get close and befriend one — they'll follow your boat forever and grant a permanent bonus:

- 🐬 **Dolphin** — +20% boat speed
- 🐢 **Sea Turtle** — +2 cargo slots

You can have one of each at a time. New wild creatures show up over time.

## Mini-Games

- 🥥 **Coconut Catch** (Home Island) — move your basket to catch falling coconuts. Avoid bombs, grab golden coconuts for bonus gold. 30 seconds.
- 🎣 **Fishing** (Reef Atoll) — click when the marker is in the green zone. 6 rounds, narrower zone each round.

## Upgrades

- **Cargo Hold** — +2 cargo slots per level
- **Faster Sail** — +25% speed per level *(unlocks Volcano Island at Lv 2)*
- **Stronger Hull** — bigger, sturdier boat
- **Lucky Net** — wider collection range *(unlocks Pearl Atoll at Lv 2)*

Upgrades scale 1.65× per level — save up for the big ones!
